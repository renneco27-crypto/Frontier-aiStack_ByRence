==============================================================
ANTI-SLOP SKILL  -  rules for making UI that does not look "vibecoded"
==============================================================
Source: video "Day 1 of Removing AI Slop" (Charles Liu), plus a
before/after on the StudyUp "Create Deck" modal.

HOW TO READ THIS FILE
- The video rules come from the on-screen captions and visuals in the
  16 frames in images/video-frames (one frame every 2 seconds).
  This is NOT a word-for-word audio transcript, only what the captions
  and the demo show. Frame 03 has the player UI covering its caption.
- Frames 04 and 05 are the "before" (the slop). The rest is the "after".

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 - WHAT THE VIDEO DOES (frame by frame)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Task: an "How old are you?" age picker.

BEFORE (frame 04, frame 05)
  - Generic slider with a tiny "29 years old" label
  - Emoji next to the title
  - Small "Step 3 of 7" chip
  - Purple gradient "Continue" button
  - Everything the same weight, nothing leads the eye
  => the default look of AI-generated UI.

AFTER (captions, in order of the demo)
  frame 01 "some snapping"           the ruler snaps to whole years
  frame 02 "Add a ..."               (animation / haptic being added)
  frame 04-05 "Make it snappable"    slider becomes a draggable ruler
  frame 06 "visual hierarchy"        ONE huge number is the focus,
                                     "years old" is small and quiet
  frame 07 "Animate the transition"  the number animates when it changes
  frame 09 "blur animation"          old digit blurs out, new one blurs in
  frame 10 "font spacing consistent" tabular digits, so the number does not
                                     jump sideways when digits change
  frame 11 "feels polished"          the result of the details above
  frame 12 "every year click"        a tiny haptic tick on EVERY step
  frame 14-15                        the ruler edges fade out
                                     ("Don't just stop" - keep refining)
  frame 16 "part 2"                  there is more to remove

THE RULES (distilled)
  1. Visual hierarchy: one thing is big and obvious, everything else is
     small and quiet. Never give every element the same weight.
  2. Snapping: controls land on clear values, they never rest "between".
  3. Animate state changes (blur / fade / slide), do not swap instantly.
  4. Consistent font spacing: use tabular numbers and fixed type sizes.
  5. Haptics: a light tick on every meaningful step or tap.
  6. Fade edges instead of hard cuts (content should not just stop).
  7. Remove default decoration: emoji as icons, gradient buttons,
     unexplained chips, glow.
  8. Keep going after "it works". Polish is the details.

--------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 - WHY MY CREATE DECK ("YOUR ORIGINAL") WAS AI SLOP
--------------------------------------------------------------
Look at: images/01-ORIGINAL-ai-slop-create-deck.png

  1. TWO CARDS SAY THE SAME THING.
     "Pro Account Required" and "Stripe Payment / Pro Account" both explain
     the paywall. The same message twice is a classic generated-UI habit.

  2. TWO COMPETING BUTTONS.
     "PURCHASE IT" and "CONNECT" are both big, filled and bright.
     No hierarchy (video rule 1). The user does not know which to press.
     "PURCHASE IT" is also an awkward label.

  3. CARDS INSIDE CARDS INSIDE A MODAL.
     Modal > dashed/solid card > another card. Nested boxes everywhere
     is padding by default, not design.

  4. ALL-CAPS BOLD ON EVERYTHING.
     Tabs, buttons and labels all shout at the same volume, so nothing
     stands out (same problem as the "before" frame in the video).

  5. GLOW AND GRADIENT PURPLE.
     Glowing borders and purple-on-purple, the default AI palette, with
     no single accent reserved for the one main action.

  6. A DEAD END.
     You open "Upload files" and the whole tab is a paywall with no
     preview of what you would get and no clear way back to the free path.

  7. TOO MANY WORDS.
     Long paragraph inside the paywall card, repeated explanations.
     Good UI says it in a few words.

  8. GENERIC ICON REUSED.
     The same credit-card icon three times (circle, button, card).

  9. SLOPPY EDGES.
     The background tab labels are clipped behind the modal, bottom nav
     labels are cut off, and the center "Create" button blends into the
     background. The details were never checked.

 10. NO MOTION OR FEEL.
     Nothing indicates animation, press states or haptics (video rules
     3 and 5).

PART 3 - WHY THE REDESIGN LOOKS BETTER (what I chose and why)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Files: html/create-deck-redesign.standalone.html  (open in a browser)
       html/create-deck-redesign.dc.html          (design-canvas source)
       html/shadlingo-design-tokens-reference.html (your design system)
Image: images/02-redesign-your-edited-version.png
       (a screenshot of YOUR edited version of the redesign. The final
       file in html/ has the later tidy-up, so it differs slightly.)

  - ONE primary action: "Upgrade to Pro" is the only large filled button.
    (rule 1)
  - ONE paywall message instead of two cards.
  - Short copy: "Pro feature", "Upgrade to Pro", "Deck title".
  - Bottom sheet sized to its content, with a grabber handle, instead of
    a floating modal with empty space.
  - Reused YOUR design system (ShadLingo / Maia tokens): Nunito font,
    #0b0c14 base, #9333ea primary, tactile 3D buttons that press down,
    16px radius inputs, pill tab switcher.  That makes the screen match
    the rest of the app instead of looking pasted in.
  - Pro badge in gold on the active tab: the only warm color, so the
    paid feature is easy to spot. (rule 1)
  - Touch targets at least 44px, muted text with enough contrast.
  - Press states on every button (tactile-btn): the 3D button moves down
    3px when tapped. (rules 3 and 5 in spirit)

HONEST NOTES / WHAT IS NOT DONE YET
  - The uppercase labels are kept ONLY because your design system uses
    them. They are a slop signal when used everywhere with no hierarchy,
    but here the hierarchy fixes it. If it feels loud, drop the uppercase
    on tabs and labels first.
  - The static design does not show motion. Still to add in the real app:
    sliding tab indicator, slide-up sheet (200-250 ms ease-out), blur
    transition when switching tabs, a light haptic on tab change and tap
    (navigator.vibrate(10) on web).
  - Not fixed in the design: the clipped bottom nav and the "Create"
    center button from the original screenshot. Those are in the app
    shell, not this modal.

--------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK CHECKLIST FOR ANY SCREEN
--------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] What is the ONE thing to look at? Is it the biggest / brightest?
  [ ] Only one primary button?
  [ ] Does any message appear twice?
  [ ] Any box inside a box that does not need to be?
  [ ] Can I cut 30% of the words?
  [ ] Are all type sizes and gaps from a fixed scale?
  [ ] Does every state change animate and every tap feel (press, haptic)?
  [ ] Any clipped, cut-off or low-contrast text?
  [ ] Does it match the rest of the app's tokens?
