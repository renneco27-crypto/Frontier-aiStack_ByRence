This Privacy Policy explains what information StudyUp collects, how we use and share it, and the choices — including deletion — you have over your data. It applies to everyone who visits or creates an account on StudyUp.

01

Introduction

StudyUp ("StudyUp," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This document describes the types of information we may collect from you, or that you may provide, when you use the StudyUp platform, and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

By using StudyUp, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

Account Data

Name, email address, password (hashed), and profile details you provide when registering.

Uploaded Content

Documents, notes, assignments, and other study materials you upload to the platform.

Usage Analytics

Pages visited, features used, session length, click patterns, and interactions with content.

Device Data

IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers, collected automatically.

03

Uploaded Content & the Shared Library

A core part of StudyUp is a shared library of study materials contributed by users — assignments, notes, and similar documents — that other students can benefit from. When you upload a file, its content and associated metadata (such as subject, file name, and upload date) may be made visible to other registered users, unless you mark it as private at the time of upload.

Do not upload documents containing other people's personal information (such as classmates' names alongside grades, contact details, or ID numbers) unless you have their permission. You are responsible for the materials you contribute to the shared library.

04

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the StudyUp platform
  • Create and manage your account
  • Organize, index, and recommend shared study materials
  • Understand usage patterns through analytics, so we can fix issues and prioritize features
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

05

Cookies & Analytics

StudyUp uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used through usage analytics. Analytics data is generally aggregated and used to improve the product experience.

You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may limit some platform functionality.

06

How We Share Information

We may share information in limited circumstances:

Other Users

Uploaded content not marked private is visible to other registered users as part of the shared library.

Service Providers

Hosting, storage, and analytics providers who process data on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.

Legal Requirements

When required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of StudyUp or its users.

Business Transfers

In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality terms.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the platform's services. If you request deletion, we remove your account data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Content you contributed to the shared library may remain on the platform in de-identified form after account deletion, consistent with the license described in our Terms of Service, unless you request its removal separately.

08

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Passwords are stored hashed, not in plain text.

⚠

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

Access Your Data Correct Inaccuracies Delete Your Data Export Your Data Object to Processing Withdraw Consent

To exercise any of these rights, see Section 12 — Deleting Your Data or contact us using the details in Section 14.

10

GDPR — EU/UK Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data under the following legal bases: performance of a contract (running your account), legitimate interests (improving the platform through analytics), and consent (where required, such as for certain cookies).

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your data has been mishandled.

11

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

StudyUp may be used by students under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children under 13 than is reasonably necessary to provide the service, and we do not use children's information for behavioral advertising.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided personal information without your consent, contact us using the details in Section 14 and we will delete that information.

12

Deleting Your Data

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us directly — see Section 14. We will confirm your request and process it within a reasonable timeframe.

ⓘ

For a full walkthrough of what gets deleted and how long it takes, see our dedicated Data Deletion page.

13

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify registered users via email or an in-app notification before the changes take effect.

Your continued use of StudyUp after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
