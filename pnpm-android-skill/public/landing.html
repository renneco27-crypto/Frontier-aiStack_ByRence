How it works

From notes to quiz in minutes

No manual card creation needed — just paste or upload and go.

1

Create a deck

Give your deck a name and tag it by subject or week. StudyUp keeps your library organized automatically.

2

Upload your material

Paste text, upload a CSV, or import from a document. Cards are generated from your own content.

3

Pick a study mode

Choose flashcards for first exposure, multiple choice to test yourself, or fill-in-the-blanks before the exam.

4

Track your progress

Spaced repetition surfaces cards you are weak on. Your streak and stats keep you accountable day to day.
